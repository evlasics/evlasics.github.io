Sometime I think it's a bit sad being in math. When I devise a cool thing, I want to show everyone I know. It's just the nature of my personality I guess. Unfortunately in math you really just can't do this. If I devise a cool result in my research, I literally cannot show it to anyone but the people I'm working with because nobody else will understand it. Compare this with other disciplines. If you are an artist, then maybe most people will not appreciate the technical inner workings and all of the clever things you do on a micro level, but they may be able to appreciate how things look at the end. Plus, many of the impressive things artists do will be apparent to the audience. I, as someone with minimal art training, can appreciate the technique that went into the works of Picasso or Monet without being an expert on visual arts. Further, as my piano teacher said, if you play music very well, then even if the audience does not consciously notice the effort you put into your voicing and phrasing, they will still notice on some level.

With mathematics nobody but you and the other people in your field will ever know how beautiful the things you've done are. Or not even just the things *you've* done, but the things that you've read and found cool. It really is quite unfortunate that nobody but those committed to spending years of their life studying will ever be able to understand these things. Alas, there is nothing to be done about this.
