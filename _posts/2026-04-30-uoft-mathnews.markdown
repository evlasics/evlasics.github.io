If you aren't aware, the university of waterloo has a wonderful monthly publication called &lquot;mathNEWS&rquot;. My university&mdash;the wonderful University of Toronto&mdash;does not, despite having (objectively) a way cooler and more awesome math program. This is depressing to me, because I love the idea of getting to publish an article every month just about random stuff. I suppose I have this blog, but I don't think anyone's really reading it to be honest. Also I'd want to read what everyone else says, and just have the experience of like reading through it all.

There's so much interesting stuff happening in the uoft math department. I don't mean math because that's a given, but rather so much interesting stuff that's *adjacent* to math. Based on their publications, the waterloo math department seems to be full of very fun and interesting people. Since our uoft math peeps are also fun and interesting, it would be great if we had a publication too to showcase this! I know that &lquot;just copying waterloo&rquot; is kinda lame, but it's okay in this case, because the more publications the better. At the end of the day it would be about making the thing rather than actually doing the math.

And during a semester it's not like people have nothing to talk about. Indeed there's been many times when I've wanted to share something with the world and haven't had the platform. Maybe we could give space for people to be more serious than waterloo while simultaneously letting people be just as silly. I think it is a real gap in the market, because math students are some of the most fun and interesting students at the university (as is to be expected of a body of students pursuing something out of passion rather than for its job prospects). If only we had more of an outlet to produce something magical. Unfortunately I'm entering my fourth year, so I wouldn't be around to give it a footing, but I'd want to see all the incredible things that it creates.

Alas, no such publication exists. I suppose I would like to try to create one, but in my slothfulness I have not. I think I just gotta coordinate with some people to make a first edition, then other people will join on when they see it and are jealous. Or maybe it would die a natural death. I don't know. But it would be worth trying, at the very least. Maybe this is a summer project.
